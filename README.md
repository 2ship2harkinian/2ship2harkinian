TLDR: A hostile takeover has happened to the 2ship repo by people who have had either very little or no involvement with the project since its inception. I (ProxySaw) am no longer involved.

## HM64 context

I have been apart of the HM64 community for a long time. I have very many sizable contributions across both Ship of Harkinian and 2Ship. If you’ve played either of these ports before, you have played with features I have implemented. If you look over to right, I am the top contributor of this repo, and I’m fairly high on the list of SoH despite not being very involved the last 2 years. I don’t say any of this to toot my own horn, it’s to demonstrate I’m not some random passerby who occasionally contributes, I’ve lived in these ports since very early on in Ship of Harkinian’s lifetime. I have held leadership positions both on the code side and on the community side.

There is a small group of individuals who are deeply rooted in the “organization” of hm64 who have made it progressively more and more difficult over the years to want to be associated with HM64, because of how unprofessional, egotistical, prideful, arrogant, or rude they are either in private or public settings. They frequently spew hatred towards other people and their projects, gossip, and as far as I know, most of their contributions in the last 2-3 years are only ones they are financially incentivized by. 

Despite all of this, I and many others have done our best to publicly suppress this unprofessional toxicity, keep it behind closed doors and even attempt to prevent it behind closed doors, eg by establishing our HM Code of Conduct and deleting an internal channel who’s pure purpose was to discuss drama. I along with many others are here to make cool stuff for people to enjoy, not to compete with or belittle other people and their projects.

Outside of this group of individuals, HM64 has been an absolutely wonderful community to be apart of, the amount insanely talented developers, artists, and extremely helpful moderators and helpers that all volunteer their free time to work on this cool thing for people to enjoy it has been such a great thing to experience and witness over these years.

## 2 Ship Context

When the 2ship project was started, some of these problematic individuals were involved in getting Majora’s Mask to compile and run on the PC. At the time, Archez and I had been working on Ship of Harkinian for a long while, we were (along with other SoH devs) in the middle of trying to put better patterns and protections in place on how to better maintain these ports, and we noticed that some of the same mistakes were being made on 2ship that bit us for a long time on SoH, so we both jumped in, and attempted to put these better patterns in place from the start on 2ship. 

This was a big uphill battle, primarily the divide was between people who were just helping with the porting process and weren’t interested in the long term maintenance of it, and the people who were going to be long term maintaining it (I’m in this group). 

One big example, was this group of individuals did not have a good relationship with the people working on the decomp, so they wanted to either completely remove the decomp history or fork from decomp and maintain our own MM decomp fork because _they did not under any circumstances want to consider decomp “upstream”_. However, in the end Archez and I won this debate, and with that, this group of individuals began to withdrawal, and Archez and I worked tirelessly together to push 2ship to the finish line. 

Since the initial release, this project has entirely been lead by four people, alternating at times because this is still all volunteer:
- Archez
- Me (ProxySaw)
- Eblo
- BalloonDude

There have also been many absolutely stellar people who have contributed as well, you can find their contributions in the history. Shockingly absent from this list is the individuals from this group of individuals outside of a commit or two over the span of 2+ years. You can pretty easily look at the 2ship channels in the HM64 discord, or at the PR history here to see who is actually involved with this project.

## So what happened?

In the recent months, this problematic behavior and drama continued to stir, at one point one of these actors without hesitation banned everyone involved with the official n64 Recomp project from the HM64 discord because they were offended about something petty. This unprofessional continued and boiled over, so I began putting into course a plan to separate the 2Ship2Harkinian project from the HM64 entity.

This plan was intended to be a peaceful transition, where the repo was moved and GitHub respected the redirect for existing links and forks, and the discord channels would become read only for a time and point to the new separate discord, and eventually be deleted from hm64’s discord entirely.

I believed I had the backup from specific people still in the HM64 organization to execute this plan, and that certain problematic individuals would be annoyed, but because they had 0 involvement in the project they would accept the project as ours and let the peaceful transition happen. I also had the backing of multiple people that had contributed to 2ship in the last 2 years, including mainly Eblo, the other Project lead.

The execution of this plan fell short, I do not blame the people I was expecting to back me up, as I maybe wasn’t clear enough with my plan with them, or something I’m not really sure what happened behind closed doors, but the reaction I got as a result of this was pure insanity. 

I had several people come out making threats and demands that in the context of open source projects (with CC0 license) just don’t make any sense, including threats of abusing the DMCA, meaning because they were offended by my actions they were willing to kill the project, showing their true colors. The strangest thing is, none of these people were involved with 2ship even remotely. Then what transpired was I was banned from the HM64 discord, they reinstated the original Github, breaking all links back to this now transferred repo, and have handed the maintainer privileges to 2 people who have never been involved with this project.

These actions obviously surprised me. To have my own project ripped from my hands from people who had 0 involvement because I wanted to distance it from the toxicity of HM64 makes me really sad.

Sure I could continue maintaining this here while HM64 maintains the official fork, but like let’s be real, this entire situation has made me nauseous about the thought of ever working on these projects again.

Goodbye for now. And to everyone apart of HM64 that isn’t in that group of individuals I truly appreciate your time and effort in that community, I love all of you even if we had our disagreements.
